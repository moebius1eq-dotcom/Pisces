Pisces

Pisces is the twelfth astrological sign in the Zodiac, originating from the Pisces constellation. It spans 330° to 360° of celestial longitude. Under the tropical zodiac, the Sun transits this sign between February 19 and March 20. In astrology, Pisces is considered a mutable sign and has a negative polarity. It is also a water sign and is ruled by the planet Neptune.
